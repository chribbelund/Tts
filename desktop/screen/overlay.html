Reading zones

Drag on the screen to draw a box around text to read. Drag a box to move it, its corners to resize it. For a cooldown or a potion count, draw a small box tight around the number and pick what it reads.

Keys
  • Tab next box, 1–9 box by number
  • Arrows move, with Shift in bigger steps
  • Alt + Arrows make wider, narrower, taller or shorter
  • W where is it (said out loud)
